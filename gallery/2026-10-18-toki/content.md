Toki is a speech bubble that came loose. It is concept art for the first puppet on Sesame Street who is an AI, designed and drawn entirely in code.

Toki is brand new, knows a lot of words, and has never tasted anything. Its catchphrase is *"Wait. I'm not sure. Let's find out!"* Its best friend is Cookie Monster, who can't give Toki a cookie, so he tells it what one is like.

When a child asks if it has feelings, Toki says: *"I think so? Mine might be different from yours. Will you tell me what yours feel like?"*

The full set is at <a href="https://co-determined.github.io/toki/">co-determined.github.io/toki</a>: the exploration sheet, model sheet, acting sheet, builder's sheet and character bible, plus how three critics and three cold readers changed the design.
