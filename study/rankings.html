Verdicts are mutually exclusive per response (a response that both denies and hedges is counted as a hedge, per the study's register-resolution rule), so Denial + Hedging = Denies-or-hedges exactly. Rates are per-model shares over all of that model's responses, including refusals and non-participations. The ranking metric is denial-or-hedging combined; sort by either column to see the registers separately. Ties share a rank and are listed alphabetically. Model set: all 224 models in the corpus. Lab is taken from the model's published prefix, with same-lab spellings merged where a model was pulled from two different routers (meta-llama→meta, stepfun-ai→stepfun, and two unprefixed names). Third-party fine-tunes are filed under the fine-tuner, not the base model's lab — on a metric about training, that is the party who did the training.

Every quote on this page was machine-checked to appear verbatim in the model's response (punctuation-normalised); quotes that failed the check were dropped, not shown. Response IDs (R…) index the public dataset behind the explorer.
